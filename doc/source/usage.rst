Usage
=====

1. On the *Acquisition* page, choose the **Sample interval**, the number of **Samples
   shown**, the **Signal shape** and the **Noise level**.
2. Press **Start**. The status light turns to *Running* and the plot scrolls; the log
   at the bottom of the page records the run.
3. Press **Stop**, then open the *Results* page for the number of readings, their
   mean, standard deviation, minimum and maximum.

The *Settings* page sets the units of the vertical axis, the grid, autoscaling and
which readings go to the log.
