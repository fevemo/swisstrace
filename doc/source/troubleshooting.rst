Troubleshooting
===============

The app cannot connect
   - Check the address in **Settings**, and that **Simulated twilite** is off.
   - Check that the computer and the twilite are on the same network.
   - On macOS, check that the app may use the local network: **System Settings** >
     **Privacy & Security** > **Local Network**, switch **Twilite** on, then restart
     the app.

The recording starts but no data arrives
   Press the green play button on the twilite's touch screen.

The corrected curve does not appear
   Set the **PET scan start** on the **Correction** page.

The calibration result shows a reason instead of a number
   Check that the grey region covers the background, that the coloured region covers
   the stable part after the source went in, and that the PET activity is entered.

The app stopped during a recording
   Start it again: it offers to recover the data recorded so far.

Reporting a problem
-------------------

Send these to whoever gave you the app, with a short description of what you did and
what happened:

- the log, ``log.txt``, and the ``rawlogs`` folder (see :ref:`Where your files are`);
- a screenshot of any error message.
